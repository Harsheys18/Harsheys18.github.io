Harshitha Inampudi — About

I'm an undergraduate in Computer Science & Engineering (Honors), with a minor in Machine Intelligence & Data Science, at IIT Bombay. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and robotics — I'm interested in how a system can perceive its surroundings, reason about them, and act reliably, especially when the environment is uncertain or only partially observable.

Most of what I've worked on so far centers on reinforcement learning under safety constraints, formal verification of learned systems, and perception-driven control for physical robots. I've adapted policy-gradient methods to respect safety constraints in continuous-control settings, looked at whether neural networks can certify their own safety guarantees, and — currently, through Sakhi and my B.Tech project — I'm working on robots where perception, reasoning, and language function as one coherent system rather than separate modules bolted together.

I'm applying to PhD programs in robotics and machine learning, hoping to keep working on problems where the goal isn't just performing well on average, but behaving correctly and predictably when it actually matters.
