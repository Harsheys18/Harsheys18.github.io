Harshitha Inampudi — About

I'm an undergraduate researcher in Computer Science & Engineering (Honors) at IIT Bombay, working on robotics, safe reinforcement learning, and formal verification. I'm applying to PhD programs in Robotics and Machine Learning.

My recent work includes Sakhi, an AI companion robot combining multimodal perception and LLM-based reasoning; a B.Tech project on perception-to-control learning with Prof. Nived Chebrolu; a safe-RL research internship with Prof. Luke Ong's group at NTU Singapore; and a constrained-RL system for Wi-Fi resource management that took the top score among all IITs at Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0. Before that, I worked across formal verification, generative modeling, quantitative research, and classical systems — xv6, PostgreSQL internals, SAT solving.

I joined IIT Bombay in 2023 after securing All India Rank 336 in JEE Advanced. Outside of research, I mentor incoming students, play table tennis, and occasionally build robots purely because it's fun — more on that in notes.

Research Interests

Robotics & embodied AI, safe reinforcement learning, formal verification of learned systems, generative & probabilistic modeling, and optimization. I'm most drawn to problems where perception, learning, and control have to work together reliably — not just perform well on average.
