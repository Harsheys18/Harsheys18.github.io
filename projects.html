Machine Learning

Reinforcement learning, generative modeling, and verification of learned systems — the second pillar of my PhD interests.

Double XGBoost: Depth-Wise Boosting

Research Problem Statement, Juspay · Dec '25–Jan '26 · work in progress toward publication

Proposed a novel algorithm introducing depth-wise boosting for improved expressivity, deriving a second-order optimization problem via Taylor series for closed-form training of node-level predictors. Demonstrated faster convergence and improved accuracy via residual correction.

RL Fine-Tuning for Diffusion Models

Advanced Reinforcement Learning · Instructor: Prof. Shivaram Kalyanakrishnan, IIT Bombay · Oct–Dec '25

Developed a PPO-based fine-tuning pipeline for text-to-image diffusion models, optimizing denoising trajectories with reward functions combining CLIP-based alignment and perceptual quality, plus timestep-level, prompt-conditioned credit assignment.

Neural Closure Certificates for Verification

R&D Course Project · Instructor: Prof. Akshay S, IIT Bombay · Aug–Nov '25

Developed neural closure certificates for verifying safety of neural networks in dynamical systems, formulating template-based certificates and training networks to learn valid certificate functions. Evaluated safety guarantees on benchmark systems including a two-room temperature model.

Probabilistic Modeling & Inference

Implemented diffusion-based generative models (DDPM & D3PM) on MNIST with low FID scores, and optimized exact and approximate FHMM inference with loopy belief propagation. Developed Bayesian optimization with GP surrogates for hyperparameter tuning in a 6D space.

Learning to Forget: Machine Unlearning

AI & Machine Learning · Instructor: Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharya, IIT Bombay · Apr '25

Implemented machine unlearning on MNIST by fine-tuning additional layers using entropy and logit suppression, preserving performance on retained data while enforcing forgetting. Integrated a confidence-based selective-prediction interface for evaluating outcomes.
