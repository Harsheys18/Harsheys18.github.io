Machine Learning

RL Fine-Tuning for Diffusion Models

Advanced Reinforcement Learning · Instructor: Prof. Shivaram Kalyanakrishnan, IIT Bombay · Oct–Dec '25

Built a PPO-based fine-tuning pipeline for text-to-image diffusion models, treating the denoising process as a trajectory to be optimized directly. The reward combined CLIP-based alignment with a perceptual-quality term, with credit assigned per denoising timestep and conditioned on the prompt, improving alignment over the un-fine-tuned baseline.

code

Probabilistic Modeling & Inference

Advanced Machine Learning · Instructor: Prof. Sunita Sarawagi, IIT Bombay · Oct–Dec '25

Moved across a few corners of probabilistic ML in one project: implementing DDPM and D3PM diffusion models on MNIST with reasonably low FID scores, optimizing exact and approximate inference for factorial HMMs via loopy belief propagation, and running Bayesian optimization with GP surrogates to tune hyperparameters in a 6-dimensional search space.

Learning to Forget: Machine Unlearning

AI & Machine Learning · Instructor: Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharya, IIT Bombay · Apr '25

Looked at whether a trained model can be made to selectively forget: fine-tuning additional layers with an entropy and logit-suppression objective so performance on retained data stays intact while the model grows less confident on data it's meant to forget. Added a small interface using a binary classifier over the softmax outputs to distinguish retained from forgotten samples, with confidence-based selective prediction for evaluation.

Markov Models for Word Tagging

AI & Machine Learning · Instructor: Prof. Pushpak Bhattacharya, IIT Bombay · Jan–Apr '25

A Hidden Markov Model part-of-speech tagger trained on the Brown Corpus, with Laplace smoothing and 5-fold cross-validation for stability, and the Viterbi algorithm handling tag-sequence decoding at inference time.

Optimization & Quantitative Research

Client-Centric Wi-Fi RRM using Safe RL

Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0, IIT Patna · Problem Statement by Arista Networks · top score among all IITs · Oct–Dec '25

As part of Team IIT Bombay, working on a problem statement from Arista Networks, I helped build a safe-RL framework for Wi-Fi radio resource management — a multi-timescale system pairing fast interference mitigation with a slower, Lagrangian-constrained TD3 loop that optimizes transmit power and bandwidth for quality of experience under safety constraints. The team's submission took the top score among all participating IITs.
