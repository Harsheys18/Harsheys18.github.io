Quantitative Researcher

Gurugram, India · May–Jul 2026

I worked on portfolio optimisation for long-short strategies, focusing on systematic weight allocation, and on evaluating machine-learning alpha signals across different model configurations — the question being less "does this signal work" than "under which configurations does it stop working". Juspay

Software Engineer — Research Problem Statement

Mumbai, India · Dec 2025–Jan 2026

I was given an open research question rather than a ticket: could a boosted-tree model gain expressivity by going deeper rather than simply adding more trees? I proposed Double XGBoost, introducing depth-wise boosting, and derived a second-order optimisation formulation via Taylor expansion so node-level predictors could be trained in closed form. Early results showed faster convergence and improved accuracy from the residual-correction step, and the work is in progress toward publication.

Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

Research Intern — Ong's Group

Advisor: Prof. Luke Ong · May–Jul 2025

A summer spent on safe reinforcement learning: adapting policy-gradient methods such as PPO and TRPO to incorporate LIDAR feedback and hazard heuristics in continuous-control settings, working inside Safety Gymnasium with 60-dimensional state spaces. The most useful part was not building something new but mapping where existing safe-RL approaches fail, and comparing them honestly against ideas from control theory that predate them.
