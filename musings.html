Musings

Not research. This is the other half — the places I have ended up because of the work, and a few I went to for no reason at all. Trains, hostel corridors, unfamiliar skylines at three in the morning, and the strange fact that almost every city I have loved I first arrived in for something entirely unromantic, like a competition or an internship.

Places

2023–present

Mumbai

Powai · home for four years now

The city I did not choose and ended up belonging to. Powai has its own weather, somehow — monsoon arrives over the lake before it reaches anywhere else. Most of what is on the rest of this site was built within about two kilometres of one hostel room.

Summer 2025

Singapore

NTU · first time living abroad

I went for a research internship and stayed curious about the city the whole time. Everything works, which is disorienting if you have grown up somewhere that improvises. The first place I lived where I knew nobody on arrival, which turned out to be its own kind of education.

Summer 2026

Delhi & Gurugram

the Graviton summer

Glass towers on one side of the highway and something six hundred years old on the other, and nobody finding this remarkable. Worked long days and spent the weekends walking through whichever ruin was nearest.

Dec 2025

Patna

Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0

Arrived for a competition, sleep-deprived, carrying a half-finished safe-RL framework. Left having seen the Ganga at sunrise, which is not what I had planned for and is the part I remember.

always

The Deccan

where I am from

Granite boulders that look balanced by hand, heat that has opinions, and food I have not found a substitute for anywhere else. The place I measure other places against, slightly unfairly.

soon

Somewhere next

undecided, on purpose

A PhD means moving somewhere I have not been, and I have decided to be pleased about that rather than anxious. This card gets replaced when I know.
