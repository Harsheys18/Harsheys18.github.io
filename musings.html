Musings

Stories Behind the Work

The rest of the site is the formal record. This corner is the informal one — short notes on what building robots, chasing research ideas, and everything outside of it has actually been like. It'll keep growing.

Robotics

Robots Outside the Lab

Between Sakhi and coursework, TerraROVER and a 3D-printed combat bot happened purely because building things by hand is a different kind of fun than proving things on paper. Motors, print tolerances, and a robot that just won't listen teach you humility research papers don't.

Origins

The Abacus Years

Long before RL or verification, there were five levels of abacus, a 3rd place at the REA Championship, and a national rank in a math olympiad. Mental math was probably the first real "optimization under constraints" problem I ever solved.

Campus Life

Sports Secretary Diaries

A year of coordinating Inter-Department tournaments taught me more about logistics, scheduling, and getting people to show up than any systems course did — badminton, volleyball, table tennis, and kho-kho, all at once.

Balance

5-Day Yoga Camps

Running Yogastha's yoga camps and breakthrough workshops was the counterweight to late nights debugging kernels and RL agents — a reminder that the calmest problem-solving happens off-screen.

Public Speaking

Master Orator

Way back at Narayana School Zonals, "Master Orator" was the title before "researcher" ever entered the vocabulary. Explaining an idea clearly out loud is still the first test I run on any new project.

Why Robotics

Why I Want Robots That Don't Feel Robotic

Working on Sakhi — getting perception, speech, and reasoning to feel like one coherent thing instead of three stitched-together modules — is what convinced me this is the problem I want to spend a PhD on.
